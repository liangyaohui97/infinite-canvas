# Product

## Register

product

## Users

AI visual creators and developers who organize prompts, references, models, generated media, and local Agent workflows on a node-based canvas. They need dense controls to remain legible while iterating quickly across many providers and model names.

## Product Purpose

Infinite Canvas brings canvas orchestration, multimodal generation, prompt management, reusable assets, and local coding agents into one workspace. Success means users can understand the current generation context, choose the intended model, and branch or reuse results without losing work or breaking flow.

## Brand Personality

Focused, capable, and calm. The interface should feel like a dependable creative tool: compact enough for expert workflows, clear enough to learn without documentation, and visually quiet enough to keep attention on the canvas.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative SaaS styling, ornamental glass effects, oversized controls, weak low-contrast text, inconsistent component vocabularies, and dense controls that hide critical identifiers behind unexplained truncation.

## Design Principles

- Keep the canvas and generated work visually primary.
- Preserve full information at the moment a user must make a choice.
- Use one consistent interaction vocabulary across nodes, workbenches, settings, and Agent controls.
- Make dense workflows scan quickly without relying on fragile hover-only behavior.
- Treat history, references, and model identity as durable user context.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain keyboard-operable controls, visible focus and selected states, readable contrast in light and dark themes, viewport-safe popovers, and reduced-motion-compatible transitions. Important model and provider identities must remain available without depending on color alone.
